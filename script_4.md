--ENGLISH--

This company sells food worth 1,400 crores without running a single restaurant.
Meet Jaydeep and Kalol, who started a rolls restaurant called Faasos. The taste was great, so the rolls became a hit, but the business kept running in losses.
But why? Because most of their orders were home-delivery. So what was the point of taking an expensive, prime-location restaurant and increasing costs?
That’s why they completely shifted their business to a simple cloud kitchen, where they started handling all customer orders with the help of Zomato and Swiggy.
And gradually, they created a separate brand for every product Oven Story for pizzas, Behrouz Biryani for biryani, and Lunch Box for meals.
In this way, from just one kitchen they created more than 15 different brands. Today, their company Rebel Foods is India’s largest virtual food court, with a valuation reaching 17,000 crores
