In the memory of his daughter, this Gujarati man almost wiped out Surf Excel.

In the 1960s, Karsanbhai Patel was extremely frustrated because the British brand Surf Excel was selling at ₹15 per kg, a price most Indians simply couldn’t afford.

That’s when he took a bold step.

Using his skills as a chemical technician, he created a simple detergent at home and named it Nirma, in memory of his young daughter who had passed away.

His smartest move? Nirma had no fragrance and no fancy packaging.

Because of this, it was priced at just one-fourth of Surf Excel.

Then came a massive turning point in 1975 with the launch of the iconic jingle
“Washing Powder Nirma” — and overnight, it became a household name.

The results shocked everyone.
Nirma snatched nearly 60% market share from Surf.

But this is where the story takes a twist.

Nirma lost focus and expanded into cement, cosmetics, and even education.
Gradually, people started seeing Nirma as a cheap brand.

That’s when Ghadi detergent took advantage of the situation.
It offered better quality and was only about 10% more expensive, giving it a slightly premium feel.

As a result, Nirma lost to Ghadi.
And by 2023, its market share dropped to just 12%.
