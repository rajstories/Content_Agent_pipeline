This company used manipulative marketing to sell an unhealthy product to Indians for nearly 40 years.
The story goes back to the 1930s, when Indians loved ghee but most people couldn’t afford it.

That’s when British giant Unilever spotted the gap and launched Dalda Vanaspati Ghee, a processed vegetable oil that worked like ghee but cost almost half the price.

With cinema advertisements, free sampling, and aggressive marketing, Dalda quickly became the top choice in Indian homes, sweet shops, and professional kitchens.
For almost four decades, Dalda faced virtually no competition.

But in the 1990s, a rumour spread like wildfire that Dalda contained animal fat. Retailers began replacing it, and at the same time, new refined vegetable oils entered the market.

As a result, Unilever was forced to sell Dalda in 2003.
