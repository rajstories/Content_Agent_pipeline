This man built a company worth thousands of crores using dry fallen leaves.
Meet Alexander. He noticed that dry leaves lying in parks and on roads were completely wasted and only added to garbage. That’s when he got the idea — instead of burning or dumping these leaves, why not recycle them into something useful?
This idea led to the launch of his company, Releaf Bags. They collect fallen leaves from parks, convert them into pulp, and use that pulp to make paper bags — without cutting a single tree.
While normal paper bags take almost a year to decompose, Releaf Bags decompose in just 30 days. On top of that, they consume much less electricity compared to traditional paper manufacturing, making them a far more eco-friendly option.
Today, Alexander’s company holds a valuation of thousands of crores, proving that waste, when used smartly, can become a massive business.
