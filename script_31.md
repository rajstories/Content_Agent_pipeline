This man who fled from Pakistan went on to build one of India’s favorite biscuit brands.
Khanchand Ramnani ran a bakery in Pakistan, but after the partition, he was forced to leave everything behind and move to Hyderabad with his family. In 1953, he had to start again from absolute zero. In memory of the city he grew up in, he named his bakery Karachi Bakery.
With no money in the beginning, he first survived by buying goods from wholesalers and selling them. In the 1960s, he returned to his true passion and started baking again. His fruit biscuits and Osmania biscuits became so popular that people lined up outside the bakery every day.
That’s when the brand took off.
Today, Karachi Bakery produces 10 tons of biscuits daily and exports its handmade products to over 20 countries.
