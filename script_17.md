A Man from Faridabad built a machine that can solve pollution, but no politician is even talking about it.

While researching Delhi’s pollution, Vidhyut discovered that nearly 20 lakh people die every year in India due to air pollution, and stubble burning alone contributes around 20% of Delhi’s smog. After speaking to farmers, he understood the real problem. Even after government fines, farmers still burn stubble because biofuel plants are far away, transportation is expensive, and there is very little time between crops. Paying the fine often turns out to be cheaper.

That’s when Vidhyut, along with his partner, launched Takachar.
A portable machine that converts stubble into fertilizer directly in the fields. This saves farmers time and cost, generates extra income, and reduces pollution by up to 95%.

Today, Takachar has worked with over 10,000 farmers and converted more than 11,000 tons of stubble into biofuel and fertilizer, generating an annual revenue of around ₹5 crore.
