Zomato has a secret business that has grown even bigger than Blinkit.
Deepinder Goyal noticed that restaurants faced a major problem sourcing fresh ingredients daily from local mandis, which were unreliable and time-consuming.

To solve this, in 2018 Zomato acquired a small startup called WOTU and relaunched it as Hyperpure. Through Hyperpure, restaurants can order raw materials directly from farmers, eliminating middlemen completely.

Since Zomato already had access to restaurant data, over 30,000 restaurants were onboarded without any marketing.

Today, Hyperpure alone generates ₹3,000 crore in annual revenue for Zomato.
