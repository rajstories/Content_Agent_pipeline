# Channel Profile

## Main Content Buckets

1. AI tools and automation
2. Finance and money-saving
3. Public awareness
4. Career and education
5. Business growth
6. Solar and electricity bill saving
7. Tech product buying decisions
8. Luxury affordability explainers

## Audience

Indian audience, mostly Hindi/Hinglish speaking.
They like useful, practical, fast, money-saving, career-saving, and smart-life content.

## Voice

Confident, direct, slightly insider-style.
No over-explaining.
No boring intro.
No "Aaj main batane wala hoon."
Start with a strong hook immediately.

## CTA Style

Comment-trigger CTA preferred.

Examples:
- "PROMPT comment karo, bhej dunga."
- "LIST comment karo, main link bhej dunga."
- "GUIDE comment karo, full breakdown bhej dunga."
