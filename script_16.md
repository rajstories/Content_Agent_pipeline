This founder turned temple flower waste into a luxury product and built a ₹200 crore company.
Ankit Agarwal from Kanpur noticed that flowers offered in temples are considered sacred, so people don’t throw them in the trash. Instead, they are immersed in rivers. As a result, more than 1,000 tons of flowers enter the Ganga every day, heavily polluting the water.
To solve this problem, Ankit quit his well-paying job in 2017 and started Phool. The idea was simple but powerful: collect waste flowers from temples and process them into premium-quality incense sticks.
These flowers are cleaned, processed, and converted into agarbattis, allowing offerings made to God to return in a purified form for worship once again.
Today, Phool has positioned itself as a luxury brand through premium packaging and high-quality incense products. The company now operates across more than five categories, generates over ₹50 crore in annual revenue, and has even received investment from celebrities like Alia Bhatt.
