Wakefit

This IIT chemical engineer built a company worth 6000 crores
Ankit Garg noticed that in India, mattresses were sold at very expensive rates despite being made from cheap quality materials, because every brand was dependent on middlemen retailers, which unnecessarily inflated the prices.

That’s why in 2016, when mattresses were sold only offline, Ankit launched India’s first online mattresses store, WAKEFIT.

Where he started manufacturing mattresses using high quality material and delivering them directly to customers, completely eliminating the middlemen cost.
And the money that used to get wasted on middlemen was given back to the customers as discounts, which made Wakefit’s mattresses high quality yet super affordable.

He also introduced India’s first 100-day trial period offer, a guarantee that helped build massive customer trust.

And then he expanded Wakefit into categories like beds, sofas, and home decor.

Today, with a valuation of 6000 crores, Wakefit has become a leading brand in the mattress and home decor category.
