This man used a legal loophole and built India’s biggest coconut oil empire.
Harsh Mariwala was handling his family’s coconut oil business when he noticed something interesting — shopkeepers would buy large tins of oil and customers had to bring their own bottles to get it measured out. It was inconvenient and messy.
So he decided to sell coconut oil in plastic pouches, but there was a problem — rats could easily chew through square packets.
That’s when he invented the iconic round Parachute bottles.
But his cleverness didn’t stop there.
He realised that hair oil was taxed at 26%, while edible oil was taxed at just 6%.
So he acquired a food license and declared Parachute as 100% edible coconut oil, saving crores in taxes — legally.
His company Marico later launched hit products like Saffola, Livon and Set Wet, and today Parachute rules the coconut oil market with a 60% share.
