## English

This Bengali businessman created a cream to fight British dominance — and today, it has become India's favourite.

Before Independence, foreign brands ruled the Indian market. Every shop sold only imported creams.

Seeing this trend, in 1929, Gour Mohan Dutta decided to create an Indian cream that could break this foreign dominance.

And that's when he created Boroline — a simple mix of boric acid, essential oils, and zinc oxide, packed in a green tube with a tiny elephant logo. While foreign creams were expensive, Boroline was made affordable for Indian consumers.

And guess what? The same cream our grandmothers used for cuts and cracks before Independence... we're still using it 95 years later.

This is Episode 1 of India's Secret Business Story. Follow for Episode 2.
