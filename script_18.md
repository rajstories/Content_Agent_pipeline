This Sindhi boy dropped out of school at the age of 15 and went on to build a 1 lakh crore electrical empire.
 After his father’s death, Inder Jaisinghani took charge of his family’s small electrical shop. In the 1970s, he realized that suppliers could change prices anytime or even stop supply altogether.
 So in 1975, he began manufacturing wires from a small garage in Mumbai and laid the foundation of Polycab.
 While competitors used cheap materials, Polycab focused on quality.
 Built on trust, Polycab expanded across India and today serves over 2 lakh retail outlets with annual revenue exceeding ₹20,000 crore.
