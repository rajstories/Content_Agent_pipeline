This 14 year old fruit seller’s son went on to shake up giants like Amul and Baskin Robbins in India.

Raghunandan Kamath, the son of a fruit vendor from Mangalore, noticed something disappointing in the 1980s. Ice cream brands were selling fruit-flavoured ice creams, but none of them actually used real fruits or milk. Everything was made with synthetic flavours.

With a dream of selling natural ice creams made only from real fruits and milk, Raghunandan came to Mumbai.

But in the beginning, he faced a big problem. People were simply not ready to visit a shop just for ice cream.

That’s when he came up with a smart idea. He started selling Mumbai’s favourite dish, pav bhaji, to increase footfall. Customers came for pav bhaji, and he offered his fruit ice creams as an after-meal dessert.

What happened next was completely unexpected.

People fell in love with his real fruit ice creams. The shop slowly turned into a crowded ice cream parlour, visited even by celebrities like Amitabh Bachchan.

Over time, without taking any loans or external funding, Raghunandan expanded steadily across India, building a profitable desi business.

Today, Naturals Ice Cream has more than 170 outlets with annual sales of around ₹300 crore.
