This is how the son of a vanaspati ghee maker became the king of the IT industry.

Azim was studying in college when his father suddenly passed away. At just 21 years of age, he had to return to India and take charge of his family’s vanaspati ghee business. Everyone believed he would fail.

But in 1977, Azim renamed the company Wipro and expanded beyond ghee into soaps, shoes, and electronics.
Then in 1978, the game completely changed when the Indian government forced IBM to exit India, even though it controlled nearly 80% of the IT market.

Azim spotted the opportunity and moved Wipro into IT services. He hired top engineers and focused on building long-term client relationships.
That decision transformed Wipro into one of the world’s leading IT giants, operating in 65+ countries and generating over $10 billion in revenue.
