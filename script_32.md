After a family partition, Haldiram’s grandson faced such a massive setback that he went on to build Haldiram’s biggest competitor. By selling bhujia at just 12 years old, Haldiram ji had built India’s largest namkeen empire by the 1960s. But after his death, the business was divided among four grandsons.
In this division, Shiv Ratan Agarwal received the smallest territory — the Bikaner region — which was only one-third the size of the Delhi division. Feeling betrayed and realizing that growth was impossible under the old restrictions, he decided to walk away from the family brand and start his own company, Bikaji.
That decision changed everything. Shiv Ratan invested heavily in technology, set up an automated bhujia factory, introduced better packaging, and even brought Amitabh Bachchan on board as the brand ambassador. As a result, today Bikaji generates over ₹2,600 crore in annual revenue and stands among the world’s biggest namkeen brands.
