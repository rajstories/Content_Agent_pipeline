This is India’s fourth largest biscuit brand, yet you may have never even heard its name.

Baijnath ji noticed that Britannia and Parle were limited mainly to big cities, while small towns and villages were either ignored or found their products too expensive.
To target this gap, he set up a small factory in Kolkata in 1994 and launched Anmol Biscuits, with a clear focus on quality biscuits at affordable prices.

For the first six years, Anmol focused only on small towns in West Bengal and the North East.
No advertisements, no celebrities—just strong distribution and low pricing.

And because of this patience and strategy, Anmol today has a presence in 34 countries and has become India’s fourth largest biscuit brand.
