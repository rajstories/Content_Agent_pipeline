People called this guy crazy for opening a vada pav shop after doing an MBA — but the same guy is now one of Burger King’s biggest competitors.
Dhiraj noticed that Mumbai eats lakhs of vada pavs every day, but mostly from unhygienic roadside stalls.
So he borrowed ₹2 lakh from friends and launched Jumbo King, a clean and hygienic vada pav outlet. People loved it so much that they soon started selling over 40,000 vada pavs a day.

But there was a challenge — running large stores is easy when you're selling ₹150+ burgers, but almost impossible with a ₹20 vada pav.
So he made a bold shift from vada pav to fresh, hygienic yet affordable burgers.

Today, Jumbo King is India’s 3rd largest burger chain with 180+ franchises and ₹100 crore+ annual revenue.
