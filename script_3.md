Doms

Doms ne is tarah Natraj ko khatam kiya. Pehle market me Natraj aur Apsara ki hexagonal pencils aati thi, jisse lamba likhne par ungliyan dard karti thi.
Isiliye Doms ne triangular pencils launch ki, jisse writing easy ho gayi, aur grooves wali pencils bhi banayi taaki grip better ho jaye.
Us time pencils ki leads graphite aur clay se banti thi jis se nib aasani se toot jaati thi but Doms ne graphite ke sath polymer use karke leads ko smoother aur stronger banaya.
Also Market me sirf red ya grey pencils milti thi, toh alag dikhne ke liye Doms ne colorful pencils launch kiye, and same cheez inhone erasers ke sath bhi kiya.
Finally, unhone pencil boxes ka shape bhi triangular kar diya taaki shelf par easily spot ho jaye.
Aur inhi smart strategies ki wajah se Doms aaj India ka fastest growing stationery brand hai.

English

Doms destroyed Natraj in this way. Earlier, Natraj and Apsara hexagonal pencils were available in the market, and writing for a long time made fingers hurt.
So Doms launched triangular pencils, which made writing easier, and also introduced grooves pencils for better grip.
At that time, pencil leads were made of graphite and clay, which made the nib break easily  but Doms used graphite with polymer to make the leads smoother and stronger.
Also, the market only had red or grey pencils, so to stand out, Doms launched colorful pencils  and they did the same thing with erasers.
Finally, they changed the pencil box shape to triangular so it could be spotted easily on shelves.
And because of these smart strategies, Doms is now India’s fastest-growing stationery brand.
