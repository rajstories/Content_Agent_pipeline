This is how Doms dethroned Camlin.
Till 2010, Camlin crayons and geometry boxes with the iconic camel logo ruled every Indian classroom. But the stationery market had become boring — same colours, same designs, no innovation. Kids wanted something stylish, something they could show off. And Doms spotted that gap.
They started innovating with pencils — boxes with different colour triangular pencils, grooved pencils for better grip, Jillion for premium users and Superior matte-finish pencils in metal boxes for the luxury segment.
One pencil was even an eraser, with a brush attached at the back.
Then came the next move — an Erasener, a two-in-one eraser + sharpener.
To reduce the hassle of buying separate products, Doms launched all-in-one stationery kits, which even became popular as birthday gifts.
With continuous innovation and strong market strategy, Doms slowly overtook Camlin and became India’s No.1 stationery brand.
