After losing almost everything, this single mother built an 8,000-crore luxury skincare empire at the age of 45.
Mira Kulkarni was forced to leave her alcoholic husband and return to her parents’ home with her children. During this phase of rebuilding her life, she had a powerful realization.
India’s markets were flooded with foreign chemical soaps, while centuries-old Ayurvedic formulations were being completely ignored.
And At the age of 45, with just ₹2 lakh invested in a small garage, she began making handmade Ayurvedic soaps using ingredients so pure they were almost edible. This became the foundation of Forest Essentials.
But purity came at a cost.
Using ultra-high-quality natural ingredients made her soaps nearly five times more expensive than mass-market alternatives. Competing on price was impossible, and for a while, it seemed like the brand might fail.
Instead of compromising on quality, she changed the game.
Mira repositioned Forest Essentials as a luxury Ayurvedic brand through premium packaging, refined branding, and thoughtful storytelling that justified its higher price. Slowly, the upper-class Indian consumer embraced the brand.
Forest Essentials products began receiving such strong word-of-mouth support that they found their way into luxury hotels, Taj properties, and even VIP rooms at Rashtrapati Bhavan. The brand then expanded into multiple categories and opened ultra-premium exclusive stores.
Today, Forest Essentials is available in over 120 countries and stands as one of India’s most respected luxury skincare brands.
