Lay’s tried twice to destroy this Indian chips brand, yet it remains one of India’s favourite snacks even today.
Back in the 1990s, Indians loved potato chips, but no one sold them with proper packaging and a consistent taste. That’s when a small Ghaziabad-based company launched Uncle Chips in 1992. The taste was so addictive that by 1998 it captured a massive 71% market share.
Then PepsiCo entered India with Lay’s, pumping crores into marketing, celebrity ads and positioning to knock Uncle Chips out. But something unexpected happened, people simply refused to switch. Demand stayed so strong that PepsiCo was forced to acquire Uncle Chips.
Later PepsiCo quietly tried phasing it out again to push Lay’s dominance, but Indians continued demanding Uncle Chips.
And that’s why even today, this brand still stands strong among India’s top-selling potato chips.
