English



These friends noticed a problem in children’s feet and created a product that has now become a ₹67-crore brand, in which Hardik Pandya has also invested ₹5 crore.


Satyajit and Kritika saw that Indian parents have to buy 4–5 pairs of shoes every year because young kids’ feet grow very quickly.


To solve this problem, they launched Aretto in 2022, with the goal of making shoes that grow along with children’s feet.


Aretto used expandable soles and 3D stretchable fabric that can shape-shift both vertically and horizontally, allowing their shoes to grow up to 3 sizes with a child’s feet. This means parents don’t have to buy new shoes repeatedly.


They also used Kushy foam, which adjusts according to the foot shape and provides extra comfort.


That’s why 5000+ parents have already adopted Aretto, and the brand has recorded 80% month-on-month sales growth, becoming a ₹67-crore kids shoe brand today.
