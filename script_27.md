This is how the creator of Parle-G made Indian kids addicted to rainbow candies.
After building hits like Parle-G and Monaco, Mohanlal Chauhan noticed something — candies in India were boring. Either plain sugar drops or sticky toffees that got stuck to teeth.
So he experimented, and came up with Poppins — an affordable rainbow candy roll that instantly captured children’s attention.
Each candy had a different colour and flavour, making kids curious and excited about what would come next. It became a playground favourite, traded and shared during school breaks.
But the genius didn’t stop there. He observed that kids loved the taste of cola but couldn’t afford cold drink bottles, so he launched Rola-Cola, a cola-flavoured candy that became an instant hit.
Today, Poppins, Melody, Mango Bite and others help Parle generate nearly ₹2000 crore+ from candies alone.
